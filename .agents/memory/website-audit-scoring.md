---
name: Website audit scoring
description: Durable rules for interpreting website audits and lead priority scores.
---

Lead priority and website quality are different signals. Priority must remain a transparent 0–100 commercial opportunity score, while technical website quality is shown separately as its own audit result.

**Why:** A missing or unavailable website is not proof that a site is slow, and a score with a lower hidden maximum makes filters and user expectations misleading.

**How to apply:** Preserve distinct `not_provided`, `unavailable`, and `checked` audit states; keep score factors and their maximums explainable; keep audit quality and response measurements visible independently.
